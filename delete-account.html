How to Request Account Deletion via Email

  • Send an email to office@worlddisastercenter.org with the subject line: "DEUS Account Deletion Request".
  • Include your full name and the email address registered to your DEUS account so we can locate and verify your identity.
  • We will confirm receipt within 5 business days and complete the deletion within 30 days, except where legal retention obligations apply (see table below).
Email: office@worlddisastercenter.org
Subject: DEUS Account Deletion Request
Include: Your full name + registered email address

What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

Some data must be retained for a period after deletion due to applicable financial regulations and anti-money-laundering (AML) laws.

Data Type Action Retention Period
Account profile (name, email, phone) Deleted None — deleted upon request
Device & usage data (IP, app activity) Deleted None — deleted upon request
Support messages and in-app communications Deleted None — deleted upon request
KYC / identity documents (passport, national ID) Retained Up to 7 years (AML / regulatory requirement)
Transaction history and financial records Retained Minimum 5 years (financial regulation requirement)

After the mandatory retention period expires, all remaining data will be permanently deleted. You will receive email confirmation once deletion is complete.

Questions

For any questions about your data or this process, contact us at office@worlddisastercenter.org. We will respond within 30 days as required by applicable data protection law.
